Low Weed Coverage

  • Continue monitoring the field for newly emerging weeds.

{% elif weed_percent < 20 %}

Moderate Weed Coverage

The analysis indicates a moderate proportion of weed pixels. Some areas may require attention to reduce competition between weeds and crops for sunlight, water, nutrients, and growing space.

Suggested actions:
  • Inspect highlighted weed regions and identify weed species where possible.
  • Prioritize areas with concentrated weed growth.
  • Consider targeted weed-control techniques appropriate for the crop and field.
  • Avoid applying treatment directly to crop plants.
  • Reassess affected areas through follow-up images.

Management priority: Targeted control and continued crop protection.

{% else %}

High Weed Coverage

The analysis indicates a relatively high proportion of weed pixels in the uploaded image. The affected regions should be inspected to confirm the extent of infestation.

Suggested actions:
  • Conduct a field inspection to verify detected weed regions.
  • Identify weed species and assess their growth stage before selecting treatment.
  • Prioritize heavily affected areas for suitable weed management.
  • Consider localized control methods only after checking crop safety and equipment suitability.
  • Monitor the field after treatment to assess remaining or emerging weeds.

Management priority: Detailed field inspection and a planned, crop-safe management approach.
